threshold. CROWDFUNDING ON OOTLE
THRESHOLD / EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOL

Fund work together.
Pay in stages.

Threshold is a crowdfunding protocol on Ootle. A community and a sponsor fund a shared goal, with payment rules agreed before anyone pledges.

The full budget must be committed before payments begin. Work can be paid in milestones, with named reviewers approving delivery. If things fall through, contributors can recover eligible unpaid funds after the deadline.

Simulation · no wallet needed
What the protocol does—and does not—guarantee

The contract enforces funding and payment rules; reviewers judge the work. Released payments cannot be reclaimed. Contributions use confidential outputs, but participation remains visible and a coordinator may learn shared amounts. Recovery needs your backup, fee funds, and network access. This implementation is testnet-only and unaudited.

Current limits & future flexibility

Current version: up to 32 pledges, 8 stages, and 5 reviewers per stage, with a sponsor share at every stage.

The design can evolve. Higher caps or sponsor-free funding need a new contract version and fresh testing against Ootle’s transaction limits. Existing campaigns keep the terms agreed before funding.

Rules for a real campaign

Recipients, sponsor, reviewers, amounts, evidence requirements, and deadlines are fixed before funding. Payments need the recipient’s signature and any required reviewer approvals before the deadline. Refunds require each contributor’s recovery backup and fee funds.

Fits v1 with a sponsor

A new software feature

Users and a sponsor pay a developer to build a feature they need.

  1. Full community + sponsor match
  2. Agreed start payment
  3. Reviewers accept the delivery

No delivery? Contributors can reclaim unpaid funds after the deadline. Money already paid cannot be recovered.

PoC template · requires agreed recipients and a sponsor

Exchange listing and integration

A community and sponsor raise money for agreed exchange fees, integration work, and testing.

  1. Agree on the work with the exchange
  2. Raise the full budget
  3. Pay at the agreed milestones

Funding does not guarantee a listing. Fees already paid cannot be refunded by Threshold.

Payment requirements

The exchange must support the Ootle signing and payment flow to receive funds directly. If someone receives the funds and pays the exchange elsewhere, you must trust them to make that payment. Threshold cannot guarantee liquidity or trading price.

Separate PoC template

Community funding without a sponsor

Members cover the whole budget for a website, event, or shared resource.

  1. Community funds 100% of the goal
  2. Milestones govern payments
  3. Owners recover eligible unpaid funds

A separate sponsor-free PoC contract is included in the repository. It is not deployed or tested end to end, and client and recovery support are unfinished. Published v1 still requires a sponsor.

PoC template · requires a sponsor

Development and a security audit

Projects and a sponsor fund a shared library and an independent auditor in one campaign.

  1. Fund development + audit together
  2. Accept the code milestone
  3. Accept the audit report and agreed follow-up

The developer and auditor can receive separate, ordered payments. Funding both does not guarantee delivery or bug-free software.

PoC template · requires a sponsor

Shared tools and maintenance

Users and a sponsor pay for wallet updates, explorer maintenance, or documentation over a fixed period.

  1. Fund a fixed work package
  2. Review progress at each stage
  3. Release the next agreed payment

Up to eight payment stages per campaign. Reviewers check the work; renewals need a new campaign.

PoC template · requires a sponsor

Community grants with a sponsor match

A foundation adds an agreed amount to community funding for education, translations, research, or open-source work.

  1. Freeze the goal and match
  2. Meet each role’s exact budget
  3. Review the agreed deliverables

One sponsor per campaign. Reviewers are chosen before funding; donating more does not buy more votes.

The additional templates are proofs of concept. Compilation and basic term checks passed; transaction, recovery, and testnet testing are still needed.

Privacy and refunds

Contribution amounts are encrypted. Participating wallets and campaign totals remain public.

To claim an eligible refund yourself, you need your saved recovery file and unlock key, funds for transaction fees, and network access.
